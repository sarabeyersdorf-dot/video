What it is

The short version.

Drop in a listing's photos (and any drone or walk-through clips), add the price and your contact info, pick a look, and export a polished video. It runs entirely on your own computer — client photos never get uploaded anywhere — and you can share this same link with anyone on the team so they can make their own.

5-minute first video

The whole workflow, start to finish.

  1. Open the app

    Use Chrome, Safari, or Edge. Bookmark the link so it's one click next time.

  2. Add your photos

    Drag 6–12 listing photos into Photos & footage. They play in the order shown — drag to reorder, or drop in a drone/walk-through clip too.

  3. Fill in the listing

    Address, city, price, beds / baths / sq ft. These appear on the opening title card.

  4. Add your branding

    Name, brokerage, phone. Set this once in the Brand Kit and it auto-fills every future video.

  5. Pick a look

    Choose a theme (Luxe, Modern, Minimal, Bold) and a format — Reel 9:16 for Reels/TikTok, Square for the feed, or Wide for YouTube/MLS.

  6. Press Export

    Preview with ▶ first if you like, then hit Export. Your video downloads, ready to post.

What you can do

Everything's optional — use what fits the listing.

Auto-montage from video

Drop several raw clips, choose a length, and the app finds the best moment in each and cuts them together for you.

Hooks & captions

A punchy opening hook — "JUST LISTED", the price, "4 BED · 3 BA" — animates on in the first second. Tap a suggestion or type your own, and add captions per photo.

Voiceover

The app writes a narration script from your listing. Record it in your own voice in one tap, or import an AI voice — it's mixed under the music.

Music & beat-sync

Drop in a track and switch on "Sync cuts to the beat" so every transition lands on the beat. Free-music links are built in.

Brand Kit

Store your logo, headshot, animated nameplate, and contact info once — it's remembered and applied to every new video.

Saved projects

Your work saves automatically as drafts and finals — photos included. Reopen and tweak anytime.

One-click multi-format

Tick Reel + Square + Wide and export all three sizes from a single build. No re-editing.

Themes & branding

Four polished themes and your own accent color, with a live preview as you build.

Pro tips

Little things that make a big difference.

Keep the tab in front while exporting. Video records in real time, so a 30-second reel takes about 30 seconds — don't switch away mid-export. Exporting three sizes takes roughly 3× as long.

Set up your Brand Kit first — then back it up. Add your logo, headshot, and contact info, hit Save, then click Back up to file. That's your permanent copy you can Restore on any computer in one click.

Aim for 20–40 seconds. Short and punchy wins on social. Lead with the exterior or an aerial, walk through the key rooms, and end on a hero shot — twilight or the backyard.

Free music, no credit required. Use Pixabay or Mixkit — links are right in the Music panel. Download a track, drop it in, and turn on beat-sync.

Good to know

A few honest limits, so nothing surprises you.

The preview voice can't be saved into the video. That's a browser limitation. To put narration in the export, use Record or Import audio — both land in the finished file.

Back up anything important. On the shared link, a browser can occasionally clear saved work between visits. Use Back up to file for your Brand Kit and Projects → Export for videos you want to keep.

Nothing uploads. Every step happens on your computer, so a client's photos stay private.
