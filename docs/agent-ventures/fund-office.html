House rules

  • No real estate, ever. No real estate services, referrals, leads or advice. Never use your name, Legacy Properties or any DRE number.
  • Spend only on its own card, within its monthly limit.
  • Ask you first before any single purchase over $100, opening any account, signing up for anything paid, or making a public claim about results.
  • No fake reviews, no spam, no copying other people's work. Follow every platform's rules, including disclosing AI-made products where required.
  • Log its money in and out here every month and write a short weekly report.
  • Grace period, review window and payouts follow the fund settings.
